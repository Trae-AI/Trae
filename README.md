# TRAE
This is the official Github of TRAE. 

## Support and Account Issues

If you have paid for a subscription but your account is locked or suspended, please follow these steps:

1. Verify your payment receipt and account email.
2. Contact support via email at feedback@mail.trae.ai with your payment receipt and account details.
3. For urgent issues, please post in the official community forum at [forum.trae.cn](https://forum.trae.cn) for faster assistance.

We do not handle account suspensions or billing issues directly via GitHub issues anymore. Please use the official support channels above.

Thank you for your understanding.
